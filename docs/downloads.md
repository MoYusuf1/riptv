# Download help

Choose your computer on the [download page](https://github.com/MoYusuf1/riptv#download-and-open).

- **Windows:** open `RIPTV-Windows.exe`.
- **Linux:** allow `RIPTV-Linux` to run, then open it. In a terminal: `chmod +x RIPTV-Linux && ./RIPTV-Linux`.
- **Mac:** unzip the download and open `RIPTV.app`. Choose Apple Silicon for M-series chips; Intel for older Macs.

The app opens your browser. Everything needed is inside the app.
Windows/Linux downloads are for 64-bit Intel/AMD computers.
Linux needs Ubuntu 22.04 or newer (glibc 2.35+); Mac needs macOS 15+.

## If something goes wrong

- **Security warning:** these apps are unsigned. Check that the download came from `MoYusuf1/riptv`; don't disable security protections.
- **Browser didn't open:** visit `http://127.0.0.1:3000`.
- **Port busy:** another copy may already be running.
- **Buffering:** try another channel. A slow provider can still cause buffering.

The app tells you when an update is ready. Close the old app and open the new download.
Use the same browser to keep your profiles.
On Windows/Linux, keep the app's terminal open while watching; Ctrl+C stops it.
On Mac, Activity Monitor can stop RIPTV if needed.
